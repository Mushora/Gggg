Everything You Need to Know

Learn more about how Mushora curates clothing and helps you discover affiliate fashion deals safely.

Mushora is a dedicated clothing and fashion affiliate discovery platform. We curate high-quality fashion essentials, contemporary tailoring, outerwear, and statement pieces from premier brands and trusted merchant partners, bringing them into one aesthetic, organized space for seamless shopping inspiration.
Our fashion team reviews and catalogs standout garments across multiple categories (such as Dresses, Outerwear, Tailoring, and Accessories). When you find an item you love, clicking "Shop Now" or the affiliate link takes you directly to the official merchant's website to complete your order safely.
No. Mushora does not stock inventory, warehouse clothing, or process customer payments directly. We act purely as a curated editorial guide and affiliate marketing portal connecting discerning shoppers to verified retailers and boutique designers.
You will complete your purchase directly on the external partner store (such as Net-A-Porter, SSENSE, Farfetch, Mytheresa, or official brand websites). All transactions, shipping, warranties, and returns are handled by the respective merchant according to their policies.
Simply click on any product card in our catalog. You will be taken to that product's dedicated details page where you can examine multi-angle high-resolution photographs, check the fabric breakdown and fit notes, review rating details, and find the direct merchant link.
While we update promotions, discount tags, and pricing guidelines frequently, product availability and final prices are determined solely by the merchant at checkout. We encourage users to verify current sizing and stock on the merchant's checkout page.
When you click "Shop Now" or the merchant affiliate button, you are redirected in a safe new browser tab to the merchant's secure product page. Mushora may receive an affiliate referral commission if you complete a qualifying purchase, at zero additional cost to you.
For partnership inquiries, brand collaborations, or editorial curation suggestions, you can reach out via email at editorial@mushora.com. For questions regarding orders, shipping, or returns of previously purchased items, please contact the merchant where you made your purchase directly.
